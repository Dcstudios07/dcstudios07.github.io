
# DC Studio Website

Welcome to the **DC Studio** website project. This is a responsive, modern, and photography-focused website built with **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**. It is designed to showcase services, projects, testimonials, and provide a contact form for potential clients.

---

## Features

- **Responsive Design:** Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
- **Home Section:** Hero banner, introductory text, and call-to-action.
- **About Section:** Details about DC Studio, expertise, and experience.
- **Services Section:** Professional photography services offered (Weddings, Portraits, Fashion, Editorial).
- **Projects Section:** Gallery of recent work with hover effects.
- **Testimonials Section:** Client reviews carousel.
- **Team Section:** Showcase of photographers and staff.
- **Contact Section:** Contact form that sends queries directly to email.
- **Smooth Scroll:** Navigation links scroll to specific sections of the page.
- **Dark, Modern Theme:** Subtle dark tones to give a professional photography feel.
